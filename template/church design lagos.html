RESIDENCE 01

Residential Architecture | Ikoyi, Lagos, Nigeria | Completed 2025

FIG. 01 — SOUTHWEST ELEVATION WITH CANTILEVERED CANOPY IKOYI, LAGOS
THE PROJECT

A contemporary residential project exploring the relationship between natural light, breeze permeability, and private outdoor space in Lagos. Designed to respond to the sub-equatorial Atlantic climate, the residence utilizes thermal mass, perforated masonry screens, and deep overhangs to achieve passive cooling.

Living spaces are orchestrated around a succession of tranquil open-air courtyards, blurring the threshold between interior seclusion and exterior lushness. The structural expression draws directly from West African modernism, prioritizing local masonry craft and resilient teak wood joinery.

JOINERY & MATERIALITY
