WARRI APARTMENT

Housing Architecture — Delta, Warri, Nigeria

A thoughtfully designed bungalow that combines functional planning, contemporary aesthetics, and comfortable living. The design emphasizes efficient use of space, well-organized interiors, and a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor environments. Careful consideration is given to natural lighting, ventilation, and the arrangement of living spaces to create a home that is both practical and inviting. With its clean architectural lines and balanced proportions, the bungalow reflects a simple yet refined approach to residential design.

LAGOS APARTMENT

Workspaces & Studios — Ibadan, Nigeria

A multi-storey apartment building that combines modern architectural design with functional living spaces. The layout prioritizes privacy, comfort, and efficient space utilization, providing well-organized residential units suited to contemporary living.

Careful attention is given to natural lighting, ventilation, circulation, and the relationship between private and shared spaces. With its balanced proportions, clean architectural lines, and practical floor plans, the building offers a comfortable and visually appealing residential environment.
